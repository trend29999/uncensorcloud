# Official releases and modified builds

Download official uncensorcloud releases from https://uncensorcloud.com/download/free.
The public release keys and current ZIP hash are available at
https://uncensorcloud.com/api/desktop/release. Compare them using a trusted browser
or independently obtained verifier, rather than trusting a modified copy's own UI.

# uncensorcloud 3.5.0 — AI mosaic removal & local subtitles

The Windows desktop edition brings AI mosaic removal (去除马赛克) and local subtitle generation into one browser workspace.

## Download and start

**[Download the full Windows package (~8.9 GB)](https://uncensorcloud.com/download/free)**

Extract the entire ZIP and run `Start-Free.cmd`. A free verified account and internet access are required for desktop authorization; no payment card is required. Windows 10/11 x64 is supported. Restoration requires a modern NVIDIA GPU and a CUDA-13-compatible driver; subtitle CPU mode is also available.

## Included in 3.5.0

- AI mosaic removal with automatic/manual regions, motion tracking, timeline corrections and temporal stability controls.
- 10-second restoration preview, synchronized comparison and full-video export with audio.
- Local Whisper large-v3-turbo transcription and M2M100 translation, with eight subtitle output languages.
- Editable cues, SRT/VTT/original-language SRT export, and switchable subtitle tracks in video exports.
- Bundled runtimes/models, hardware checks and a synthetic processing-time benchmark.

Local videos and transcripts stay on the computer. Model outputs should be reviewed and do not guarantee recovery of original information. Public examples are synthetic, non-person test fixtures.
